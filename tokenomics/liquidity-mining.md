# Liquidity Mining

{% hint style="warning" %}
Designed for the v3 deployment; not yet re-published for the current protocol version.
{% endhint %}

63% of the initial supply of DER is distributed to the community via Liquidity Mining, Trading Incentive, and Referrer Commission. To participate in Liquidity Mining, you need to follow the following steps:

1. Provide liquidity by depositing into the [Vault](../liquidity/vault.md) serving the incentivized markets, receiving VaultLP shares in return. The incentivized markets are listed with each program.
2. Lock the VaultLP into the Liquidity Mining contract and earn the DER [rewards.md](rewards.md "mention") along with the LP [funding-rate.md](../protocol/funding-rate.md "mention") and, where the reserve is a yield-bearing token such as rETH or BNBx, its underlying yield.
